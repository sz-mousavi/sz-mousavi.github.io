About

Molecular geneticist and senior bioinformatician specializing in infertility genomics, multi-omics integration, and biomarker discovery.

As a postdoctoral researcher at the Technical University of Munich, I lead the bioinformatics component of Ph.D. projects in head-and-neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC). I have extensive experience in family-based genotype–phenotype correlation analysis for male infertility and previously headed the Bioinformatics department at TuCAGene, translating multi-omics data into clinically relevant findings.
