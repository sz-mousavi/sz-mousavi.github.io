About

I work at the intersection of genomics and bioinformatics: multi-omics for male infertility, cancer biology, especially head-and-neck and pancreatic cancer, and extracellular vesicle biology for liquid biopsy.

At TUM, I lead the bioinformatics side of HNSCC projects and run work on plasma extracellular vesicle subpopulations. Before that, I headed the Bioinformatics department at TuCAGene, taking multi-omics projects from design through interpretation and reporting, while mentoring junior analysts.

Ph.D. in Molecular Genetics, Tarbiat Modares University, with a research visit at TUM.
